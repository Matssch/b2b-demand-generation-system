# B2B Demand Generation System
A practical framework for turning market opportunity into qualified demand, Sales opportunities and measurable pipeline.

## Overview
B2B demand generation is not simply about generating more leads. The objective is to create and capture demand from the right companies, move that demand through the buying journey and connect Marketing activity to pipeline and revenue. The system I use is:

**Market → ICP → Segmentation → Demand → Engagement → Qualification → Sales → Pipeline → Revenue**

Each part of the system affects the next:
+ More traffic does not solve a targeting problem.
+ More leads do not solve a qualification problem.
+ More MQLs do not solve a weak Sales handoff.
+ And more campaigns do not automatically create more pipeline.

This repository shows how I would structure a B2B demand generation engine around the complete commercial journey.

## Demand creation vs demand capture
A healthy demand generation engine needs both.

### Demand creation
Reaching companies and buyers before they are actively looking for a solution. Examples:

- Paid social
- Thought leadership
- Educational content
- Events
- Partnerships
- Account-based campaigns
- Category/problem awareness

### Demand capture
Converting existing buying intent into a commercial journey. Examples:

- Paid Search
- SEO
- High-intent landing pages
- Comparison pages
- Demo requests
- Contact forms
- Retargeting

The two work together.

**Demand creation builds future opportunity.**

**Demand capture converts existing intent.**

## Segmentation before campaigns
Different customers should not automatically receive the same buying journey.A useful starting model is:

### Smaller accounts
Primarily digital and self-service.Marketing carries most of the acquisition and conversion journey.

### Mid-market accounts
Hybrid journey. Marketing creates and captures demand, while Sales becomes involved when intent or opportunity value increases.

### Larger accounts
More Sales-led and account-focused. Marketing supports awareness, education, stakeholder engagement and opportunity progression.

This creates a progression from:

**Lead generation → Segment-based demand generation → Account-based growth**

rather than forcing every account into the same funnel.

## The demand generation engine
The operating model is:

### 1. Market
Where is the commercial opportunity?

### 2. ICP
Which companies are most likely to create value?

### 3. Segmentation
Which buying journey fits each type of customer?

### 4. Demand
How do we create and capture buying interest?

### 5. Engagement
Which accounts and people are showing meaningful signals?

### 6. Qualification
Which demand deserves further investment?

### 7. Sales handoff
When and how should Sales become involved?

### 8. Pipeline
Is Marketing creating real commercial opportunity?

### 9. Revenue
Which activities contribute to customers and commercial growth?

## Measurement principle
The reporting model should move beyond lead volume. The funnel becomes:

**Target accounts** → **Engaged accounts** → **Qualified demand** → **Sales accepted** → **Opportunities** → **Pipeline** → **Customers** → **Revenue**

Channel metrics still matter, but they are diagnostics underneath the commercial outcome.

## Core principle
**Demand generation is not about creating the largest funnel. It is about creating the highest-value flow of qualified commercial opportunities.**
