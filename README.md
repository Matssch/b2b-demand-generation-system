# B2B Demand Generation System
**A practical B2B growth framework connecting market opportunity, ICP, demand creation, qualification, Sales alignment and pipeline.**

## The idea
B2B Demand Generation is not about generating the maximum number of leads. The objective is to create and capture demand from the right companies and convert that demand into: **Qualified opportunities → Pipeline → Revenue**

The system is:

**Market → ICP → Segment → Buying Group → Demand → Engagement → Qualification → Sales → Pipeline → Revenue**

This repository shows how I would structure that system from end to end.

---

## The commercial question
Instead of asking: How do we generate more leads? I start with: **How do we create more qualified pipeline from the companies that matter?** That changes how Marketing operates. Traffic, clicks, CPL and MQLs remain useful. But they become diagnostic metrics underneath the commercial outcome.

---

## Demand Generation System
### 1. Market opportunity
Understand where customer need, commercial value and ability to win overlap.

### 2. Ideal Customer Profile
Determine which companies deserve Marketing and Sales investment.

### 3. Segmentation
Match the GTM motion to customer economics.

**Smaller → Self-service**
**Mid-market → Hybrid**
**Strategic → Sales-led / Account-based**

### 4. Buying groups
Understand who inside the account influences the decision.

### 5. Demand creation
Build awareness before active buying intent exists.

### 6. Demand capture
Convert existing intent into the correct commercial journey.

### 7. Campaign architecture
Connect: **ICP → Problem → Message → Channel → Experience → Qualification**

### 8. Account scoring
Prioritise accounts using: **Fit + Intent + Engagement + Buying Group + Commercial Value**

### 9. Sales alignment
Define when Sales should act and create a closed feedback loop.

### 10. Pipeline measurement
Measure Demand Generation through: **Target Accounts → Engagement → Qualified Demand → Sales Accepted → Opportunities → Pipeline → Revenue**

---

## Demand creation vs demand capture
A healthy B2B growth engine needs both.

### Demand Creation
Create relevance before the buyer is actively searching. Examples:
- Paid Social
- Thought leadership
- Events
- Webinars
- Partnerships
- ABM
- Educational content

### Demand Capture
Convert active buying intent. Examples:
- Paid Search
- SEO
- Solution pages
- Comparison content
- Retargeting
- Demo requests

The system becomes: **Create demand → Capture intent → Qualify → Convert to pipeline**

---

## Segment-based GTM
Different customer economics require different growth motions.

| Segment | Motion | Marketing role | Sales role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smaller | Self-service | Acquire + convert | Limited |
| Mid-market | Hybrid | Create + qualify | Moderate |
| Strategic | Account-based | Create + influence | High |

This avoids forcing every account through the same funnel.

## Practical pipeline model
This repository includes a synthetic demand generation dataset to demonstrate the measurement system. Example modelled results:

| Segment | Spend | Opportunities | Pipeline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Small | €17K | 38 | €190K |
| Mid-market | €30K | 36 | €840K |
| Strategic | €37K | 21 | €1.295M |

The important lesson: **The segment producing the most opportunities is not necessarily the segment creating the most value.**

## Measurement hierarchy
### Commercial outcomes
- Revenue
- Pipeline
- Customers
- Win rate

### Opportunity
- Opportunities
- Average opportunity value
- Cost per opportunity
- Pipeline per € invested

### Sales alignment
- Sales accepted demand
- Sales acceptance rate
- Time to follow-up

### Demand
- Qualified accounts
- Engaged accounts
- Buying-group engagement

### Channel diagnostics
- Spend
- Reach
- CTR
- CPC
- CPL
- Conversion rate

The hierarchy prevents channel metrics from becoming the business objective.

## About the data
The dataset used for the practical analysis is **synthetic**. It is designed to demonstrate the commercial logic, measurement structure and decision-making framework without using confidential company information. The framework combines principles I have used in segment-based marketing, acquisition, Sales alignment and full-funnel growth with the more advanced B2B Demand Generation operating model I would use in a modern SaaS environment.

---

## Core principle
**Demand Generation is not about building the largest funnel. It is about creating the highest-value flow of qualified commercial opportunities.**

Marketing should not end at the lead. It should understand what happens all the way through:
**Market → Demand → Sales → Pipeline → Revenue → Learning**
