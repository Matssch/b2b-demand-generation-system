# Demand Generation Commercial Analysis

## Purpose
This analysis shows how I would use demand generation data to make commercial decisions. The dataset is synthetic and exists to demonstrate the measurement framework used throughout this repository. The objective is not to optimise for the largest number of leads. The objective is to understand:

**Where is Marketing creating the strongest commercial opportunity?**

---

## Overall performance
Across the modelled campaigns:
- Marketing investment: **€84K**
- Qualified accounts: **314**
- Sales accepted accounts: **256**
- Opportunities: **95**
- Pipeline created: **€2.325M**
- Customers won: **36**
- Revenue: **€875K**

Sales acceptance rate: **256 / 314 = ~82%** 
Opportunity conversion from Sales accepted demand: **95 / 256 = ~37%**
Cost per opportunity: **€84K / 95 = ~€884**
Pipeline generated per €1 of Marketing investment: **€2.325M / €84K = ~€27.7**

These metrics should not be interpreted independently. Segment economics change the value of each opportunity.

---

## Finding 1 | Small accounts generate volume
The Small segment generated:
- €17K Marketing spend
- 38 opportunities
- €190K pipeline

Average opportunity value: **~€5K**
Pipeline per €1 invested: **~€11**

This segment generates significant opportunity volume. The commercial model should therefore remain highly scalable and efficient.

### Decision
Prioritise:
- Search
- SEO
- CRO
- Automation
- Self-service conversion

Avoid unnecessary Sales cost.

---

## Finding 2 | Mid-market creates stronger pipeline economics
The Mid-market segment generated:

- €30K Marketing spend
- 36 opportunities
- €840K pipeline

Average opportunity value: **~€23K**
Pipeline per €1 invested: **~€28**

Opportunity volume is similar to the Small segment, but commercial value is substantially higher.

### Decision
Increase focus on:
- Qualification
- Paid Search
- LinkedIn
- Webinars
- Case studies
- Sales alignment

The objective should be qualified pipeline rather than maximum lead volume.

---

## Finding 3 | Strategic accounts create the highest value
The Strategic segment generated:

- €37K Marketing spend
- 21 opportunities
- €1.295M pipeline

Average opportunity value: **~€62K**
Pipeline per €1 invested: **~€35**

This segment generates the fewest opportunities but the largest amount of pipeline.

### Decision
Do not judge strategic campaigns using lead volume. Investment can justify:
- ABM
- Executive events
- Buying-group campaigns
- Personalisation
- Sales coordination

because the underlying customer economics are different.

---

## Finding 4 | ABM can look inefficient until pipeline is measured
The ABM pilot generated only: **8 opportunities**
But those opportunities represented: **€480K pipeline**
Average opportunity value: **€60K**

A lead-generation dashboard might make the campaign look small. A pipeline view tells a very different story. This demonstrates why Demand Generation should be evaluated beyond conversion volume.

---

## Finding 5 | Sales acceptance is a useful quality signal
Approximately **82%** of qualified demand was accepted by Sales. This is an important feedback metric. If Marketing increases qualified-account volume but Sales acceptance falls significantly, I would investigate: 
- ICP quality
- Targeting
- Scoring
- Intent thresholds
- Segment mix
- Campaign quality

More qualified demand is only valuable when the definition of "qualified" holds up commercially.

---

## Finding 6 | Budget should follow marginal opportunity
Historical performance does not automatically determine the next investment. Before increasing budget, I would ask:
1. Can the campaign scale?
2. What is the expected marginal cost?
3. Will opportunity quality remain stable?
4. Is Sales able to absorb additional demand?
5. What is the expected pipeline value?

This prevents budget allocation from becoming: Give more money to whichever channel currently has the cheapest lead.

---

## Commercial conclusion
The data illustrates the central principle of this repository: **Volume ≠ Value**
A strong B2B demand generation engine needs to connect: **Account fit** + **Intent** + **Qualification** + **Sales acceptance** + **Opportunity value** = **Commercial priority**

The strongest Marketing decision is therefore not always the campaign producing the most leads. It is the campaign or segment creating the strongest sustainable flow of valuable pipeline.
